The Look collection

Sixty-two ways to find a feeling.

A few useful details

Before you
step outside.

Can I download Aki Camera now?

Not yet. Aki Camera is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone, where it will be listed as “Aki Cam: Film & Vintage Camera”.

Are the pictures on this page made with the Looks?

Yes, with one caveat. They are existing photographs processed by the same Look engine the app uses, at 100% strength; they were not taken with Aki Camera. A Look adapts to each scene’s light, so your results will differ.

How many Looks are there?

62, in seven collections: アキ (8), リン (11), Daily (5), Film (10), Cinematic (10), B&W (6) and Seasons (12).

Do Looks work for video too?

Yes. Looks are live in the viewfinder for both photos and video, and their strength can be set from 0 to 150%.

What do the camcorder sets add?

Softer tape colour and a camcorder display with a blinking REC light, tape counter, battery mark, and the date and time in period fonts. You choose what is burned into saved video and photos: the date stamp, the camcorder display, or both. There is camcorder sound for recorded audio as well.

What happens when I export?

The photos and videos you select are added to Photos. The copies stay in Aki Camera’s gallery.

Does Aki Camera include advertising?

Yes. Exporting may show a short rewarded ad, and a banner ad appears at the bottom of the gallery. There are no ads on the camera. If no ad is available, your export goes ahead.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account, and your photos and videos are not uploaded. Aki Camera asks only to add to Photos, when you export. See the privacy policy.

Which iPhones are supported?

iPhone X through the latest models, including iPhone Duo, with iOS 16 or later. Lenses, video options, photo size and Camera Control depend on the iPhone.

Where can I ask a question?

Visit Aki Camera support or email akistudio.mobile@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model and what you would like help with.
