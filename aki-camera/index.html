Questions

Before you step outside.

Can I download Aki Cam now?

Yes, as a public beta. Install Apple’s TestFlight app on your iPhone, then open the Aki Cam beta link to join. Aki Cam: Film & Vintage Camera is coming soon to the App Store.

Do Looks work for video as well as photos?

Yes. All 62 Looks are live in the viewfinder for both photo and video.

What do the camcorder sets add?

Each of the five sets — Handy ’96, Wide DV ’03, Night ’99, Pocket ’05 and Shoulder ’89 — adds a REC display, a timecode, a battery mark, tape colour and period sound. Shoulder ’89 is part of Basic; the other four are part of Aki Cam Pro.

Are ProRes and Apple Log available on every iPhone?

No. ProRes and Apple Log are available on supported iPhones only.

What is Basic, and what is Aki Cam Pro?

You can use Aki Cam in Basic without a purchase: the Daily and B&W Looks, the Shoulder ’89 camcorder set, the Black Mist lens filter, date stamps and all the camera basics. Aki Cam Pro adds the アキ, リン, Film, Cinematic and Seasons Looks, the other four camcorder sets, the other six lens filters, manual ISO, shutter, white balance and focus, and ProRes and Apple Log. You can preview every Pro item live; the shutter asks for Pro only if a Pro item is in your shot. Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Does Aki Cam show ads?

No. Aki Cam has no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and it collects no data.

Where do my photos and videos go?

Straight to your Photos library. If you do not allow Photos access, they are saved to Files › On My iPhone › Aki Cam instead. There is no in-app gallery.

Were the pictures on this page taken with Aki Cam?

No. They are existing photographs processed by the app’s own Look and camcorder engines, and the phone screens are app screenshots showing those photographs. The camcorder, date stamp, lens filter and Pro panels you can play with here are browser sketches, so results in the app will differ.

Where can I get help?

See the support page or read the privacy policy.
