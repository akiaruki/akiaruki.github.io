Aki Camera privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

No data collected

Aki Camera collects no data. It has no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and it contains no advertising or analytics software. There is no account or sign-in, and nothing about you or your photos is sent to Aki, to advertisers or to anyone else.

Your camera, photos and videos

Aki Camera processes photos, videos, Looks and date stamps on your iPhone. Camera permission enables the viewfinder and capture. Microphone permission is requested for videos with sound; you can record without sound. Your photos, videos and recorded audio are not uploaded anywhere.

Where your photos and videos are saved

Aki Camera has no in-app gallery. Every photo and video is saved straight to your Photos library, for which the app asks permission to add items (add only; it does not ask to read your Photos library). If you do not allow that, items are saved to Files › On My iPhone › Aki Cam instead. Once saved they belong to Photos or Files and stay there until you delete them. They may sync through iCloud Photos or iCloud Drive if you use those Apple services.

Location and other data

Aki Camera does not request your location, contacts or any other personal data, and it does not add location to your photos. Your settings, such as the Look you chose, are stored only on your iPhone.

Purchases and Aki Cam Pro

Aki Cam Pro is bought through the App Store as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Apple processes the payment. Aki Camera never receives your payment details; the app receives only Apple's verified purchase status, so it knows whether Pro is active. Subscriptions are managed, cancelled and refunded through your Apple Account and Apple, under Apple's terms and privacy policy.

Contact and privacy requests

Aki Camera is provided by Aki. For support or privacy questions, contact akistudio.mobile@gmail.com. If you email us, we receive your address, your message and any attachments, and use them only to answer your request. Changes to this policy will update its date.
