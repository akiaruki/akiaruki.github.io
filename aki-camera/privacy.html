Aki Camera privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-19

Your camera, photos and videos

Aki Camera processes photos, videos, Looks and date stamps on your iPhone. Camera permission enables the viewfinder and capture. Microphone permission is requested for videos with sound; you can record without sound. Aki Camera has no account or sign-in, and it does not upload your photos, videos or recorded audio to Aki or to advertisers.

The app's gallery

New photos and videos are kept in the app's own gallery on your iPhone. They stay there until you delete them in the app or delete the app. The gallery is part of the app's data, so it is included in your iPhone backups according to your device's backup settings. Deleting the app removes its gallery.

Exporting to Photos

When you export, Aki Camera asks for permission to add items to your Photos library (add only). It does not ask to read your Photos library. Exported photos and videos are copies: they also stay in the app's gallery, and they stay in Photos until you delete them there. They may sync through iCloud Photos if you use that Apple service.

Location and other data

Aki Camera does not request your location, contacts or any other personal data, and it does not add location to your photos. Your settings, such as the Look you chose, are stored only on your iPhone.

Advertising

Aki Camera shows ads from Google AdMob in the gallery: a banner at the bottom of the gallery and, when you export, a rewarded ad. If no ad is available, the export continues. Ads never appear on the camera screen and never block saving your photos. Your photos and videos are never passed to the advertising SDK.

Google's data and ad settings

Aki Camera requests non-personalized ads only and does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission. This does not make advertising anonymous. Google's SDK may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and device identifiers such as the identifier for vendors. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Your advertising choices

Where the law requires it, Google's consent form asks for your choices before ads are requested, and the app offers Ad privacy in Settings when Google requires that entry point. The options you see depend on your region. Google handles information it receives under its own privacy policy.

Contact and privacy requests

Aki Camera is provided by Aki. For support or privacy questions, contact akistudio.mobile@gmail.com. If you email us, we receive your address, your message and any attachments, and use them only to answer your request. Changes to this policy will update its date.
