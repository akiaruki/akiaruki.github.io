Aki Camera support

Updated 20 November 2026

Which iPhones are supported?

iPhone with iOS 16 or later, from iPhone X to the latest models, including iPhone Duo. Lenses, resolutions, frame rates and photo sizes follow what your iPhone supports.

How do I change the Look?

Swipe the Look strip under the viewfinder, or open the collections menu: アキ, リン, Daily, Film, Cinematic, B&W and Seasons. Tap the strength button to set the Look from 0 to 150%. On iPhone with Camera Control, turn it to change Looks.

How do camcorder sets work?

Choose a camcorder set to record with a vintage camcorder look: softer tape colour, a REC light, tape counter, battery mark and the date and time. In Settings you choose what is burned into saved videos and photos: the date stamp, the camcorder display, or both.

How do I add a date stamp?

Turn on Date stamp in Settings and choose the style, font, colour and corner. You can also set a fixed date of your choice.

Where are my photos and videos?

Aki Camera has no in-app gallery. Every photo and video is saved straight to your Photos library, so open the Photos app. If you did not allow Photos access, they are saved to Files › On My iPhone › Aki Cam. You can change Photos access in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.

What is Basic, and what is Aki Cam Pro?

Basic needs no purchase: the Daily and B&W Looks (and Original), the Shoulder '89 camcorder set, the Black Mist lens filter, date stamps, every resolution and frame rate, and all the camera basics. Aki Cam Pro adds the アキ, リン, Film, Cinematic and Seasons Looks, the other four camcorder sets, the other six lens filters, manual ISO, shutter, white balance and tint, and focus, and ProRes and Apple Log video on supported iPhones. You can preview every Pro item live in the viewfinder; the shutter asks for Pro only if a Pro item is in your shot, and Switch to Basic turns those items off.

How do I buy, restore or cancel Aki Cam Pro?

Open Settings in the app, or press the shutter with a Pro item in your shot, and choose Monthly, Yearly or Lifetime (Lifetime is a one-time purchase and is not offered in every country). Payment is charged to your Apple Account, and the App Store shows the price before you confirm.

To restore a purchase after reinstalling or on a new iPhone, open the Aki Cam Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account.

Monthly and Yearly subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. To manage or cancel, open iPhone Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions and choose Aki Cam. You keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for. Family Sharing is not available for Aki Cam Pro. Apple handles payments and refunds; to ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does Aki Camera show ads or collect data?

No. Aki Camera has no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and it collects no data. Read the privacy policy.

The camera says it is unavailable.

Check that Aki Camera has camera access in Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera, and close other apps that are using the camera. Video records without sound if microphone access is off.
