A few useful answers

What is Basic, and what is Aki Studio Pro?

You can download Aki Studio and use Basic without a purchase. Basic includes a set of Basic Looks, Develop, Crop, Detail, Frame and Date, and full-resolution JPEG export with no watermark. Aki Studio Pro adds all 62 Looks in seven collections, Curves, Color Mixer, B&W Mixer, Split Toning, Optics, Geometry, Effects, thirteen light and film effects, batch color grading of up to nine photos, .cube LUT and .aki Look import, and PNG and WebP export. You can try Pro Looks and tools on your own photo before you buy. Exporting with Pro edits asks for Pro, or lets you save a copy without them.

Where are my photos and projects stored?

Editing happens on your device. Projects stay in the app's local storage, and exported copies go to the Photos or Files location you choose. Aki Studio has no cloud project backup, so keep a separate copy of important photographs.

What if a photo won't import or save?

Check the app's Photos permission in your device's Settings and make sure there is space available on your device. If the issue continues, tell us the file format, whether you used Photos or Files, and any message shown in the app.

Are there ads?

No. Version 1.11.1 removed advertising entirely. The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Our Privacy Policy explains how your photos and purchases are handled.

How do I buy, restore or cancel Aki Studio Pro?

Open Aki Studio Pro in the app and choose Monthly, Yearly or Lifetime (Lifetime is a one-time purchase and is not offered in every country). Payment is charged to your Apple Account, and the price is shown by the App Store before you confirm. There is no free trial.

Monthly and Yearly subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. To manage or cancel, open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Aki Studio. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for.

If you reinstall the app or use a new device, open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds for App Store purchases, so we cannot refund them directly. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com or use Apple Support, signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Pro does not unlock, or I was charged twice. What should I do?

First tap Restore Purchases in the app with the Apple Account that bought Pro, and check your connection. If Pro is still not active, email us with your app version, device model and what you see, but please do not send payment details. Apple Support can help with billing questions.

How do I report an issue?

You can send feedback through TestFlight or email support. Include your installed app version, device model, photo format, any error message, what you expected, and what happened instead. Please check Photos or your chosen Files location for the saved copy before repeating a save after an unexpected interruption.
