Privacy Policy

Your photographs are personal. Aki Studio has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not collect personal data.

Aki Studio is available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. This policy covers the iOS app (version 1.11.1 and later), this website, and support requests sent by email.

Your photos and edits

Aki Studio processes the photos you choose, their metadata, and your editing instructions on your device. It does not upload that content to an Aki Studio server, and it has no account system.

Projects are kept in the app's storage on your device. Saving an edited copy to Photos or Files leaves your original separate. Aki Studio does not provide cloud sync or cloud backup for projects. Copies you save to Photos or Files may be synced by Apple or another service according to your own settings.

The app opens only the photos you choose, using Apple's photo picker. It also has add-only access to Photos so it can save copies you export. You can manage the app's Photos permission in your device's Settings.

No ads, no tracking

Aki Studio contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking software, and it does not collect personal data. Nothing about you or your photographs is sent to Aki Studio, to advertisers, or to data brokers.

Earlier versions of the app (before 1.11.1) included Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform, and Google's services could process information such as IP addresses, device identifiers and ad interactions as Google describes in its own policies. Version 1.11.1 removes that software entirely. If you are still using an earlier version, update the app from the App Store.

Purchases and Aki Studio Pro

Aki Studio Pro is bought through the App Store as a Monthly or Yearly subscription or a Lifetime purchase. Apple processes the payment. Aki Studio never receives your payment details, and the app checks whether Pro is active on your device using Apple's purchase information.

Apple may provide us with standard sales and subscription reports. These are anonymized and are not used to identify you. Subscriptions are managed, cancelled and refunded through your Apple Account and Apple, under Apple's terms and privacy policy.

Storage and deletion

You can remove projects in Aki Studio. Copies you have separately saved to Photos or Files remain in those locations until you manage or delete them there. Keep a separate copy of important photographs; app storage is not a backup service.

Aki Studio does not hold a server copy of your projects to retrieve or delete, and it keeps no personal data about you.

When you contact support

If you email us, we and our email provider receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond and investigate the issue. Use a non-sensitive sample if you need to share a photo, and only include details needed to explain the problem.

For questions about your information or to request deletion of a support message, email akistudio.mobile@gmail.com.

Testing through TestFlight

Apple handles TestFlight distribution and testing information. Depending on your invitation and the feedback you submit, we may receive testing activity, crash reports, contact details, messages, and attachments through TestFlight. We use that information to investigate issues and improve Aki Studio.

Only attach photographs you are comfortable sharing when sending feedback. Apple's TestFlight & Privacy notice explains what Apple collects and shares with developers.

This website

The tool arrangement demo remembers your chosen order in this browser's local storage. Clear this site's browser data to remove it. Look previews use prepared images; the website does not ask you to upload your photographs, and it has no advertising or analytics scripts.

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to operate its services. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for its practices.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the app's features or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest version. For help with the app, visit Support.
